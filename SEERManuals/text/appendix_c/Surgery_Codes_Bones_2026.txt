A250 Local excision

[SEER Note: According to the CoC, "excision" in the surgery codes refers to the lesion and "partial
resection" refers to the organ.]

A260 Partial resection

[SEER Note: According to the CoC, "excision" in the surgery codes refers to the lesion and "partial
resection" refers to the organ.]

A300 Radical excision or resection of lesion WITH limb salvage

[SEER Note: Assign code A300 when the tumor was excised and the limb was saved (salvaged).
     Example: Six cm sarcoma excised from soft tissue near the distal humerus. Able to obtain 2 cm
     pathologic margins. Plastic team intercepted to perform graph with muscle taken from abdomen.]
