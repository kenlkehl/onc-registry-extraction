                                             Surgery Codes

                                                Testis
                                              C620–C629

Codes

A000 None; no surgery of primary site; autopsy ONLY

A120 Local tumor destruction, NOS
No specimen sent to pathology from surgical event A120

A200 Local or partial excision of testicle

A300 Excision of testicle, WITHOUT cord
[SEER Note: Orchiectomy not including spermatic cord.]

A400 Excision of testicle WITH cord or cord not mentioned (radical orchiectomy)
[SEER Note: Orchiectomy with or without spermatic cord.]

A800 Orchiectomy, NOS (unspecified whether partial or total testicle removed)

Specimen sent to pathology from surgical events A200-A800

A900 Surgery, NOS

A990 Unknown if surgery performed; death certificate only
