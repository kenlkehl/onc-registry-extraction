Laterality

Laterality must be coded for all subsites except carina.


Pancoast Tumor

Pancoast tumor is a lung cancer in the upper-most segment of the lung that directly invades the brachial
plexus (nerve bundles) of the neck, causing pain. It is by definition malignant. Code the date of diagnosis
from the imaging report when a Pancoast tumor is identified on imaging prior to biopsy.
