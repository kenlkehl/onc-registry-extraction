                                         Coding Guidelines

                                  Kaposi Sarcoma of All Sites
                                           (M9140)

Primary Site

Kaposi sarcoma that is not AIDS-related is a rare condition. It usually presents as localized disease with
an easily recognized primary site.

AIDS-related Kaposi sarcoma usually presents as a disseminated disease with involvement of mucosal
surfaces, visceral surfaces of organs, and skin. It is important to review consecutive records carefully to
determine the extent of involvement at diagnosis. Review of a single record may reveal only the site being
treated during that admission.

      1.     Code the Kaposi sarcoma to the primary site in which it arises.
      2.     If the Kaposi sarcoma is present in the skin and another site simultaneously, code to the
             specified skin site (C44_).
      3.     If the primary site is unknown or cannot be determined, code skin, NOS (C449).
