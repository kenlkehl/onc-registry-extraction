                                         Coding Guidelines

                                   Renal Pelvis and Ureter
                                Renal Pelvis C659, Ureter C669

Primary Site

Urothelial cell carcinoma originates in the urethra, bladder, ureters, and renal pelvis. Code the primary
site to renal pelvis (C659) when urothelial cell carcinoma originates in the “kidney.”


Laterality

Laterality is required for sites C659 and C669.
