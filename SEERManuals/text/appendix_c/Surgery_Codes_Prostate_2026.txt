A500 Radical prostatectomy, NOS; total prostatectomy, NOS
Includes excision of the prostate, prostatic capsule, ejaculatory ducts, seminal vesicle(s); and may include
a narrow cuff of bladder neck.

A700 Prostatectomy WITH resection in continuity with other organs; pelvic exenteration
Surgeries coded A700 are any prostatectomy WITH resection in continuity with any other organs. The
other organs may be partially or totally removed. Procedures may include, but are not limited to
cystoprostatectomy, radical cystectomy, and prostatectomy.
[SEER Note: “In continuity with” or “en bloc” means that all of the tissues were removed during the
same procedure, but not necessarily in a single specimen.]

A800 Prostatectomy, NOS

Specimen sent to pathology from surgical events A200-A800

A900 Surgery, NOS

A990 Unknown if surgery performed; death certificate ONLY
