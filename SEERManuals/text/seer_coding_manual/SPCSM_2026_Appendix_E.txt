    27 Conjunctival primary acquired melanosis (PAM) with         According to our expert pathologist consultant, there has been a lot of debate in the literature about the diagnostic criteria,
       atypia                                                     terminology, and natural history of PAM. The main issue is whether PAM with atypia should be regarded as melanoma in situ. In
                                                                  most studies it appears that PAM with no atypia or mild atypia does not progress to melanoma, and only a small percentage of
                                                                  those with severe atypia do so. PAM, even with atypia, is not melanoma in situ, and should not be reported.
                                                                  For further information, see this article for a review of a large number of patients: Shields, Jerry A, Shields, Carol L, et al. Primary
                                                                  Acquired Melanosis of the Conjunctiva: Experience with 311 Eyes. Trans. Am Ophthalmol Soc 105:61-72, Dec 2007.
